# About Retrobun

Hi, I’m Jenny. Welcome to **Retrobun**.

This blog is all about my love for retro games and the amazing community of collectors who keep these classics alive. Here, I share guides, stories, and tips on:

- **Collecting** retro consoles, cartridges, and accessories
- **Spotting fakes** and bootlegs in the wild
- **Repairing and preserving** classic hardware and carts
- **Documenting history** so it does not fade away

I started Retrobun because newcomers to collecting often have the same questions: what is real, what is fake, what is worth saving, and what is still fun to play. These guides are here to help you build a collection with more confidence.

Thanks for visiting. If you have stories, tips, or discoveries of your own, I would love to hear them.
